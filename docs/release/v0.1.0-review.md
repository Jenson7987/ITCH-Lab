## Known limitations

- Official source files and bulk derived runs cannot be distributed; reproduction requires
  separately authorised files matching the recorded identities.
- The official study uses one day per chronological partition, so confidence intervals requiring
  at least five test days are correctly omitted.
- Historical visible-book simulation excludes hidden/off-venue liquidity, market impact, price
  improvement and live counterfactual participant behaviour.
- Release signing is deferred; published SHA-256 checksums detect corruption but are not a digital
  signature.
